Meet Your Dentist

Dr Mark Killen

Owner & Principal Dentist

Dr Mark Killen is well known for his kind and gentle manner, guaranteed to calm even the most anxious patient. His commitment to informative and ethical dental care ensures you receive the best possible results.

Mark studied his Bachelor of Science at the University of New South Wales in 1998, completing an Honours year in Pathology. He then earned his Bachelor of Dental Surgery with Honours from the University of Sydney in 2003.

After building a solid foundation at Westmead Hospital alongside specialist practitioners, Mark spent over a decade mastering the latest dental technologies — delivering results that are both exciting and relaxing for patients.
