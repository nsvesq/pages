Hooking up the Joyo MOMIX
  1. Guitar into the MOMIX's instrument / guitar input. Headphones into the MOMIX.
  2. Connect the MOMIX to the phone or computer (USB-C if yours has it, otherwise its phone cable).
  3. Tap Plug in guitar, allow the microphone, then choose the MOMIX under Input.
  4. Don't feed the phone's sound back into the MOMIX inputs (Line In / loopback). If you do, the backing track goes through the amp sim and gets distorted.
  5. Too much lag? Turn Hear the amp off and listen to the dry guitar on the MOMIX. The recording still gets the amp tone.
